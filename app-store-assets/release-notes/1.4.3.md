# What’s New in Sleep Detector 1.4.3

More reliable, clearer and more accessible.

- Clear All Data now removes everything, including scheduled reminders, and nothing reappears afterwards.
- Your sleep estimate is saved as soon as you open the app, so a night is not lost if iOS closes the app during startup.
- Coming back after several days away no longer suggests one multi-day “sleep”. Only the most recent night is estimated, as a rough guess.
- The review card says “Possible sleep” when the estimate is uncertain, its time buttons dim when an adjustment isn’t possible, and the wake time can no longer be moved into the future.
- Apple Health duplicate protection now recognises sleep periods Sleep Detector has already written, and Apple Watch nights are no longer stored twice.
- Statistics, Today and sleep details share one sleep-quality scale, and Today’s summary updates correctly at midnight and on daylight-saving days.
- VoiceOver now describes the settings button, the time adjustments and every switch.
- Faster screens, less background storage work, and a smaller app.

Your sleep and wellness data stays private and is processed on your devices.

1.4.2 was withdrawn from App Review before approval, so this text also covers its changes; App Store users update straight from 1.4.1.
