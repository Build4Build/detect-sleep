# What’s New in Sleep Detector 1.4.3

Clearer estimates and a more accessible app.

- Coming back after several days away no longer suggests one multi-day “sleep”. Sleep Detector now estimates only the most recent night and tells you it is a rough guess.
- The review card says “Possible sleep” when the estimate is uncertain, and its time buttons dim when an adjustment isn’t possible. The wake time can no longer be moved into the future.
- Statistics use the same sleep-quality scale and colours as the rest of the app.
- Wellness notes show sleep in hours and minutes and speak to you directly.
- VoiceOver now describes the settings button, the time adjustments and every switch.
- Apple Watch nights are no longer stored twice after a refresh.
- Tidier test notification, export and Clear All Data messages, and a daily tip that really changes daily.

Your sleep and wellness data stays private and is processed on your devices.
